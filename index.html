Math practice

Free math practice for students, teachers and parents. No ads, no accounts, no internet.

What it does

  • Choose topics – arithmetic, fractions, decimals and percentages, units of measure – and set the limits for each one.
  • Practice with feedback after every answer, or take a test and see the results at the end.
  • Save setups and share them with students as a file or a QR code.
  • Follow progress in the history and statistics, and export them to Excel.
  • Print worksheets with an answer key.
  • In English and Lithuanian.
