Privacy statement

Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short: Mathisnas collects no data. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no internet access. What you create stays on your device.

Who we are

Mathisnas is made by IT Tech, MB (company code 306963885), Eduardo Andrė g. 14-5, LT-02232 Vilnius, Lithuania. You can reach us at info@ittechgo.com.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts or sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It does not ask for permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your settings, your setups and the history of your practice sessions are stored only on your device, inside the app. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Files you share

You can export your history (as a CSV or Excel file), share setups (as a file or a QR code), save a backup of all your data and print worksheets (PDF). These files are made only when you choose to, and you decide where they go. If you send them through another app, that app's own privacy rules apply.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan the QR code of a setup. The app asks the first time you scan. The picture is read on your device and is not saved.
  • Vibration: a short buzz for right and wrong answers. You can turn it off in the app's settings.

Children

Mathisnas is made for children and students as well as adults. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Google Play

When you download Mathisnas from Google Play, Google handles your data under its own privacy policy. From Google we may receive anonymous statistics, such as the number of installs, and crash reports from people who allowed this in their phone's settings. These do not tell us who you are.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics, and it loads nothing from other websites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages: GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to keep the service secure, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Your rights

As we hold no personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to show you or to delete. If you have a question, write to info@ittechgo.com. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country (in Lithuania: the State Data Protection Inspectorate).

Changes

If this statement changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
